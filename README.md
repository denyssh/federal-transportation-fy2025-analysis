Federal Transportation FY2025 Analysis

Project Overview
Analysis of U.S. federal contract spending in the Transportation & Warehousing sector (NAICS 48) for Fiscal Year 2025.

Data Source
USAspending.gov

Dataset
5,013 federal contract transaction records.

Tools & Methods
- Microsoft Excel
- Power Query
- Data cleaning and transformation
- PivotTables
- Data visualization
- Contractor and geographic spending analysis

Key Findings
- Total FY2025 federal obligations analyzed: $2.64 billion.
- The top 10 contractors received approximately $1.98 billion, representing 74.7% of total obligations.
- Amentum Services, Inc. was the largest contractor in the dataset with approximately $473.2 million in obligations.
- Virginia was the leading state by place of performance with approximately $688.6 million.
- The top 10 states accounted for approximately 86.4% of total obligations.

Deliverable
Interactive Excel analysis containing cleaned transaction data, contractor rankings, state-level analysis, KPI metrics, and visualizations.
