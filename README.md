# Federal Transportation FY2025 Analysis

## Project Overview

Analysis of U.S. federal contract spending in the **Transportation & Warehousing sector (NAICS 48)** for Fiscal Year 2025.

The project uses federal contract transaction data from **USAspending.gov** to identify leading contractors, geographic concentration of spending, and key procurement trends.

## Dataset

- **Source:** USAspending.gov
- **Fiscal Year:** 2025
- **Sector:** Transportation & Warehousing (NAICS 48)
- **Transactions analyzed:** 5,013
- **Federal obligations analyzed:** $2.64 billion

## Tools & Methods

- Microsoft Excel
- Power Query
- Data cleaning and transformation
- PivotTables
- KPI analysis
- Data visualization
- Contractor ranking
- Geographic spending analysis

## Key Findings

- Total FY2025 federal obligations analyzed: **$2.64 billion**
- The top 10 contractors received approximately **$1.98 billion**, representing **74.7%** of total obligations
- **Amentum Services, Inc.** was the largest contractor in the dataset with approximately **$473.2 million** in obligations
- **Virginia** was the leading state by place of performance with approximately **$688.6 million**
- The top 10 states accounted for approximately **86.4%** of total FY2025 obligations
